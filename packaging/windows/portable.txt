Open3D Launcher portable mode

While this file sits next to open3d-launcher.exe, Open3D Launcher keeps its settings, its
download cache and its record of installed apps in the Open3DLauncherData folder beside it, and
writes nothing to %APPDATA%. The programs it opens are told to keep their files in that folder
too. Copy the whole folder to move Open3D Launcher with its settings.

Delete this file to use the normal per-user folders instead: %APPDATA%\Open3D\Launcher for the
settings and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Open3D\Launcher for the rest.
